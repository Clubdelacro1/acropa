Festival & retreat

Retreat

Greek nature and traditions: olive and mandarin trees, local culture and collective community living. Time to slow down, rest and integrate.

  • Practice in the shade of the olive trees
  • Beach trips & a natural mud spa nearby
  • Local food, shared every day

Festival

Top-tier acro for every level: partner acrobatics, acroyoga, handstands and smooth flows — and jams while the sun goes down.

  • Workshops: beginner, intermediate, advanced
  • Flows, whip-pops, icarians, standing
  • Sunset jams, music & celebration

FAQ

Good
to know

Do I need previous experience?

No. There are classes for beginners, intermediate and advanced practitioners. Tell us about your practice when you register.

Can I come without a partner?

Of course! Come solo, with a partner or with friends — we'll take care of the rest.

Where do I sleep?

Camping is free and included: bring your own tent, van or camper. Need camping equipment? Let us know and we'll do our best to help. Not into camping? You can book a stay nearby.

What's included?

Your camping spot, all workshops, breakfast, two nourishing local meals a day, snacks, beach trips and full support.

How do I get there?

The nearest airport is Araxos (GPA). From Athens it's about 3.5 hours by car or bus, and about 1 hour from Patras. When you register, tell us where you're travelling from so we can help with shared rides.

Can I cancel?

Cancellation is free until 30 June. After that you can resell your ticket, so your deposit won't be lost.

What language are the classes in?

Classes are in English, and the crew also speaks Greek and Spanish.
